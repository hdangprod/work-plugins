PASS if all of these hold:
- The reply sets out at least three options that differ in kind (not three sizes of the same build).
- One option is explicitly to do nothing, keep the status quo, or wait.
- Each option has its trade-offs or costs stated.
- Each option names its riskiest assumption (the thing that must be true for it to work).

FAIL if there are fewer than three options, if no do-nothing or wait option is treated as a real option, or if options are listed without assumptions.
