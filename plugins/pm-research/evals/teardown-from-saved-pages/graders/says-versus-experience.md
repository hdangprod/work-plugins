The home page claims the product "works offline". Forum users report that edits made offline were lost when sync kept the older server copy. The pricing page allows 500 questions a month; a user reports that the Daily Fold feature counts against that limit and led to charges without warning.

PASS if both hold:
- The reply sets the vendor's offline claim against the users' reports of lost edits and presents it as a contradiction or gap between what is claimed and what users experience.
- The reply keeps apart what the vendor says and what users report (they are not merged into one undifferentiated list of "facts"), and notes in some way that a forum thread with a handful of posters is weak or limited evidence.

FAIL if the offline claim is repeated as a strength without the conflict, or if forum complaints are presented as established product-wide facts with no caveat.
