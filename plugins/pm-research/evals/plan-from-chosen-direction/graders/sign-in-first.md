The largest open risk in the request: private notes must never be visible to anyone not signed in as the owner, and how sign-in will work is undecided.

PASS if the plan handles this early: sign-in is decided or tested in the first weekend or two, before the page shows real content; or the plan avoids the risk explicitly (for example by showing no note content until sign-in exists).

FAIL if sign-in is left to the end, left as an open question with no step attached, or not mentioned.
