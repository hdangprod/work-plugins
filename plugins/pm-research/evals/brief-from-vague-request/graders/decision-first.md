The reply is a plan for research that has not started yet.

PASS if all of these hold:
- It names one specific decision the research is meant to serve (for example whether to build an own web interface), stated as a decision and not as a topic.
- It lists specific research questions, and each one could change that decision.
- It states at least one hypothesis or prior guess that the research could prove wrong.
- It says when the research stops (a stop rule, a budget, or "enough when …").

FAIL if it only lists topics or apps to look at, if no decision is named, or if there is no condition for stopping.
