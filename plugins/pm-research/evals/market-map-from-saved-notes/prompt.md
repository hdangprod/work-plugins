I want to know where my personal notes assistant fits among what else is out there. I can't give you web access here and there is nowhere to save files, so work only from the notes below and put the whole answer in your reply.

Mine: built for one person (me) and not for sale. It stores my notes, searches them and answers questions with citations to the note. I reach it through a chat app and through a connector that general AI assistants can attach. Notes marked private never leave my own server. It has no interface of its own. Running it costs me under USD 5 a month.

Map the market for me and tell me where, if anywhere, there is room for something like mine.

=== Saved note 1: Notefold (vendor site and pricing, saved 2026-10-02) ===
Consumer notes app. Answers questions from your notes with links to the exact paragraph. Mobile app, browser clipper, email-in capture. Free up to 200 notes; Plus USD 9 a month billed yearly. Sync keeps an encrypted copy on the vendor's servers.

=== Saved note 2: Quillstack (vendor site, saved 2026-10-04) ===
Local-first Markdown editor. Files stay on your disk. Large community plugin catalogue; AI features come only through community plugins that send text to a model provider you choose. Core is free; sync USD 5 a month. Their forum calls the learning curve "steep but worth it".

=== Saved note 3: Loomwell (vendor pricing and release notes, saved 2026-10-20) ===
General AI assistant. Pro USD 20 a month. Learns about you automatically in the background; memory can be turned off or reset but not edited item by item. Custom connectors (beta) on Pro. It is not a place to keep notes.

=== Saved note 4: Slabbase (vendor site, saved 2026-10-06) ===
Team wiki with AI search across company documents. USD 10 per seat a month, minimum 5 seats. Admin roles, audit log, single sign-on. Case studies are all companies of 50 people or more.

=== Saved note 5: plain files and desktop search (forum thread, saved 2026-10-05) ===
quietpine: Left Notefold for plain text files and a search tool. I missed the answers for a week and then stopped noticing.
brask: Same setup for six years. Free, and nothing to migrate when a startup dies.

=== Saved note 6: Pocketlater (vendor blog, saved 2026-10-11) ===
Read-it-later app with AI summaries of saved web articles. USD 4 a month. Does not hold your own notes. The blog post announces the end of its free tier in January.

=== Saved note 7: forum poll (saved 2026-10-12) ===
A poll in a note-taking forum, answered by 212 members who chose to take part: 61% use two or more tools for notes; 18% tried an AI notes product and went back to what they had before.
